Frequently asked questions

What can I encode in a QR code?

QuickQR supports website URLs, plain text, WiFi settings, WhatsApp links, email actions, phone numbers and basic contact information.

Do I need an account?

No. This version is designed to let visitors generate basic QR codes without registration.

Can I print my QR code?

Yes. Download the PNG and test it from the intended print distance before producing a large quantity.

Why does the scanner need HTTPS?

Modern browsers protect camera access and generally require a secure context such as HTTPS or localhost.

Can this website make money?

Yes, once you have real traffic you can evaluate legitimate monetization such as display advertising, sponsored placements or paid business features, subject to the relevant platform policies.
